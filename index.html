FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to file a return in India as an NRI?+

If your Indian income exceeds the basic exemption limit, or you want a refund of excess TDS, you should file. Many NRIs file only to recover tax deducted on interest or rent.

How much TDS is deducted when I sell property?+

Buyers deduct TDS on the capital gain at NRI rates, often on the full sale value. A lower-deduction certificate can reduce this to the tax actually payable.

What does a retainership cost?+

The fee depends on your business size and the scope agreed on the first call. It is fixed monthly, so there are no surprise bills.

Can I consult remotely from abroad?+
